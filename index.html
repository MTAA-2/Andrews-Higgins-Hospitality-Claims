Why Hospitality Workplaces

Environments That Can Enable Abuse

Conditions in hospitality work can create opportunities for misconduct — especially when employers fail to keep employees safe:

Late-Night Shifts

Hospitality work often runs late into the night, with fewer people around.

Isolated Work Areas

Employees may work alone, or away from others, for long stretches.

Alcohol Service

Workplaces built around alcohol service can heighten the risk of misconduct.

Power Imbalances

Significant power imbalances between employees and supervisors can be exploited.

Unsafe Workplaces

Employers who fail to maintain a safe workplace put employees at risk.

Cover-Ups

Businesses, or their representatives, may cover up prior sexual assault allegations against the perpetrator.

Fear of Retaliation

Many survivors delay reporting because they fear retaliation or losing their job.

Not Being Believed

Embarrassment, and fear of not being believed, keep many survivors silent.

Adult Survivors

California Law May Still Allow Your Claim

What We Look At

If you experienced sexual abuse, sexual assault, or repeated workplace sexual misconduct as an adult while employed in California's hospitality industry, compensation may be available through a civil claim.

  • Where you worked — a restaurant, bar, hotel, catering company, event venue, or other California hospitality workplace.
  • What happened — sexual abuse, sexual assault, or repeated workplace sexual misconduct, experienced as an adult.
  • When it happened — less than 1 year ago, 1–2 years ago, 3–4 years ago, or over 4 years ago.
  • What the business knew — including whether the business, or its representatives, covered up prior allegations against the perpetrator.
